Our mission

Education shouldn't depend on your postcode

Since 2014, Stackly Rural Education Foundation has partnered with local communities to build schools, train teachers who stay, and put age-appropriate books into small hands — in the language children actually speak at home.

How a school gets built

From first visit to first school bell

A typical rollout takes eleven months, start to first day of class.

01

Community assessment

We spend two weeks in the village, mapping how many children are out of school and why.

02

Local hiring

We recruit and train teachers from within a 10km radius — people the children already trust.

03

Build & equip

Classrooms, latrines, a well and a small library go up using local contractors.

04

Handover & monitoring

The school council takes ownership; we visit quarterly for the next five years.
